COFFEE RECIPE☕

Ingredients

  • 1.5 tsp Instant coffee
  • 1.5 tsp Sugar
  • 1 Cup Milk

Procedure

  1. Mix in a Cup, combine Sugar and Instant Coffee with hot boiled water
  2. Whisk is vigorously until it starts to foam
  3. Boil milk in a separate pot
  4. Add the hot milk in the cup of coffee mixture while stirring it till it blends
  5. Sprinkle some instant coffee on top and serve

And enjoy your hot Coffee☕
