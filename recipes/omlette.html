OMLETTE RECIPE🥚

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Curry leaves and Garam masala
  • 2 tsp Oil of your choice
  • A block of Butter

Procedure

  1. Crack two eggs in a bowl
  2. Whisk those eggs and add some salt to that
  3. While whisking the eggs, heat up some oil in a pan
  4. After the oil starts to heat, add a block of butter to that
  5. In the pan, add pepper, Garam masala and Curry leaves and after a min, add the egg mixture to the pan
  6. Spread the mixture using a metal or wood spatula
  7. Make sure the omlette doesn't sticks to the pan and then flip it
  8. Now, increase the heat and let the other side cook well.
  9. After the other side cooks well, turn of the heat and flip it again.

you can enjoy your omlette with some good toastes
