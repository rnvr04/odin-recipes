CHAI RECIPE ☕

Ingredients:

  • 2 tsp Chai Patti
  • 2 tsp Sugar (or to taste)
  • 2 Cups Milk
  • 1/4 Cup Water
  • 1 Ginger stick

Procedure

  1. Take some water in a pot and bring it to heat
  2. Add sugar and chai patti to the water and let it boil
  3. After getting enough heat add warm milk to the pot and let it soak the chai flavours
  4. After boiling it, add grated ginger to the milk and let it boil
  5. Let it boil for a while, strain it in cups and serve.
